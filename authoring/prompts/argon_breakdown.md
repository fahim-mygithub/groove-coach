# Argon Video Breakdown Prompt

This prompt is handed by the orchestrator (via Herdr / Antigravity) to **Argon** (Gemini video understanding model) along with the ingest kit generated by `yt-ingest`.

---

## 1. Input Kit

You are provided with three assets:
1. **`proxy.mp4`**: Low-resolution (<=480p) video of the source footage preserving the original audio and framerate.
2. **`contact_sheet.jpg`**: A contact sheet showing 1 frame per second with burned-in timestamp watermarks (`ss.ms`).
3. **`ingest.json`**: Technical metadata including duration, resolution, audio channels, and preliminary shot cut candidates detected by scene difference analysis.

---

## 2. Objective

Your task is to analyze the dance choreography and return a **machine-checkable JSON breakdown** describing:
- Musical tempo (BPM), first downbeat, and phrase cadence.
- Dancers in frame, designating one **target dancer** to track.
- Camera stability across shots, flagging cuts or camera motion that degrade 3D pose estimation.
- A recut plan (time trim + crop bounding box + horizontal flip) to extract a clean, single-subject video.
- Atomic dance moves with counts, difficulty, and anatomical focus.
- 8-count phrase-aligned segments for interactive coaching.
- A pedagogically sound teaching sequence.
- Occlusion windows (e.g. clasped hands, face covers) where joint tolerances must be scaled.
- Capture-quality notes (lighting, contrast, mirrors).

---

## 3. Strict Rules

1. **Target Dancer**:
   - Choose exactly **one** primary dancer to track throughout the lesson (`target_dancer_id`).
   - Provide bounding boxes `[t_s, x, y, w, h]` in pixel coordinates at multiple representative timestamps across the clip.
   - Specify a `crop` box in source pixels framing this dancer so `num_poses = 1` pose trackers stay locked on them.
2. **Shot Stability & Usability**:
   - Pose tracking (MediaPipe PoseLandmarker and Apple Vision 3D) requires steady, static framing.
   - Mark shots with camera movement, pan/zoom, or cuts as `usable: false`.
   - Never place a teaching segment across a cut or inside an unusable shot.
3. **Phrase-Aligned Segments**:
   - Segment boundaries must align to the music's 8-count phrases (or 4-count half-phrases when a clear bridge exists).
   - Segments must partition the active choreography in chronological order without gaps or overlaps.
4. **No Invented Beats**:
   - Anchor the beat grid to the first clear kinematic and audible downbeat (`first_downbeat_s`).
   - Every segment's `start_s` and `end_s` **must land within ±40 ms** of the calculated BPM grid ($t = \text{first\_downbeat\_s} + k \times \frac{60}{\text{bpm}}$).
5. **Teaching Order (Pedagogical Progression)**:
   - Rank segment IDs in `teach_order` (e.g. `["C", "A", "B", "D", "full"]`).
   - Rule of thumb: Teach easiest first, **unless** the routine has a distinct "hook" or signature movement that benefits from early mastery (e.g. hook groove taught first, followed by intro, bridge, finale, and full run).
6. **Occlusion & Clasp Windows**:
   - Identify moments where the hands clasp (e.g. *Namaste* / *Anjali mudra*), cover the face, or cross the torso.
   - Mark the time window and specify the affected canonical joints (`leftWrist`, `rightWrist`, `nose`) so the engine applies tolerance scaling.
7. **Free-Text Move Names**:
   - Move names are free-text descriptions (e.g., `"Right Ear Flick & Left Hip Pops"`). Do not force into any predefined enum.
8. **JSON Only**:
   - Return **strictly valid JSON** matching the schema below. No conversational preamble or trailing commentary.
